Hypotheses

The hybrid architecture combining classical convolutional embedding with circular data re-uploading (L=3, 4 qubits) provides superior non-linear representation compared to single-shot angle encoding and to a classical linear baseline operating on the identical 4-dimensional representation.
Circular CNOT entanglement topology, which entangles every bipartition of the 4-qubit register without making all of them maximally entangled (no 4-qubit state is maximally entangled across its three two-versus-two bipartitions at once, as proposal r04 states), yields higher diagnostic discriminability than linear nearest-neighbor entanglement or separable non-entangled single-qubit rotations.
Expressivity in the accessible Fourier spectrum increases with re-uploading depth L up to an optimal bound, beyond which gradient variance decays (barren plateau effect) and causes training stagnation or overfitting on validation folds.
Analytical gradient saliency derived from the classical backbone and quantum parameter-shift rule concentrates its mass on electrophysiologically relevant fronto-central channels and Theta/Beta frequency components rather than posterior noise.
With a fixed feature front end, the re-uploading readout (L=3) represents a class rule that needs Fourier frequency two in one feature, which single-shot angle encoding (L=1) cannot represent; when the class signal is monotone every arm stays close to the known ceiling, and its degradation with the training label-noise rate is measured.

Conclusions

This work introduced the hybrid quantum-classical data re-uploading network (Hybrid DRU), a classifier for electroencephalography (EEG) screening of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).
On the general task, the Hybrid DRU matched but did not surpass its comparators.
For clinicians and reviewers, the consequence is a screening model whose sensitivity can be traced to electrodes and whose reported performance is a property of unseen children rather than of recordings the model has already seen.
Future work follows three directions motivated by the limitations of this study.
